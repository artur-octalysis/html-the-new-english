Our contribution protocol

Build. Share. Continue.

01 / MAKE IT WORK

One folder per team.

Work in your assigned pair folder. Improve index.html and update your README with your motivation hypothesis and checks.

02 / MAKE IT REVIEWABLE

Open a pull request.

Explain your motivation hypothesis, what changed, and how you tested it. Ask the original authors to review extensions.

03 / MAKE IT VISIBLE

Merge, publish, present.

After your changes merge and deployment finishes, your version appears here automatically. Unchanged handout copies stay out of the showcase.

This site and its repository are public. Use fictional content and keep secrets and client data out of submissions. Prototypes run in a restricted viewer: scripts work, but pop-ups, form submissions, and access to the gallery are blocked. Read the contribution guide.
