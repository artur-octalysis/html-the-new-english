# Brand implementation notes

Based on the user-supplied “The Octalysis Group — Brand protocol”, version 0.1, 15 September 2026. The Brand Hub remains the source of truth; this implementation does not claim approval.

- Headings: Poppins. Body: Libre Franklin. Loaded through Google Fonts, with local Arial fallback when unavailable. No claim is made about a separate corporate font license.
- Established colors: #00428F, #005CC2, #0078FA, #A6E3FF, #F29E36, #F2911C. White and light neutral surfaces support readable navy text. Orange marks emphasis rather than large decorative backgrounds.
- The September proposal colors were not promoted to the established palette.
- The company name is plain typeset text. No official logo, octagon, or framework artwork was generated or redrawn. An original approved logo can be added later.
- No stock imagery, invented client evidence, or fabricated outcomes were introduced.
- The gallery's shared UI follows the brand; prototype content and colors are preserved as original work.
- Motion remains purposeful and can be paused. Reduced-motion settings are respected.
